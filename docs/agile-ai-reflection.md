# AI in FitPlan Agile Planning

## What AI helped draft

In this project, AI was used to prepare draft requirements, test cases, user stories, sprint plans, estimates, and Agile practice notes. These drafts need to be checked and adapted by the student team.

## Benefits and challenges to evaluate

| Area | Possible benefit | Possible challenge | What our team observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| User stories | AI can suggest story wording and acceptance criteria quickly. | Suggestions may include assumptions users did not confirm. | Fill in after review. |
| Estimates | AI can provide a starting point for discussion. | Estimates may not match the team’s skills or project setup. | Fill in after planning poker. |
| Sprint planning | AI can group related stories into a draft plan. | Dependencies, time limits, and team availability may be missing. | Fill in after planning. |
| Testing ideas | AI can suggest edge cases to consider. | Some tests may be duplicates or rely on rules not yet agreed. | Fill in after running tests. |
| Documentation | AI can help organize project notes. | Drafts can sound complete even when evidence has not been collected. | Fill in after reviewing the report. |
