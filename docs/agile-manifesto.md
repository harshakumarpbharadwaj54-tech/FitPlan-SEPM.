# Agile Manifesto and Principles for FitPlan

## Four Agile values

| Agile value | FitPlan practice |
|---|---|
| People and communication matter more than relying only on processes and tools. | Hold short team check-ins and review changes together. |
| Working software matters more than producing documentation alone. | Aim to demonstrate a working increment, such as profile or workout logging, during each sprint. |
| Collaboration with users matters more than relying only on an initial agreement. | Ask users or classmates for feedback and update the backlog when requirements are unclear. |
| Adapting to new information matters more than following a fixed plan. | Revisit priorities when testing or user feedback reveals a problem. |

## Twelve principles applied to FitPlan

| # | Principle in simple words | FitPlan practice |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Deliver useful software early and keep improving it. | Build profile and logging before adding weekly plan generation. |
| 2 | Accept reasonable changes, even late in development. | Update the backlog if feedback reveals a missing requirement or safety concern. |
| 3 | Deliver working pieces frequently. | Review a working increment at the end of each one-week sprint. |
| 4 | Keep users and developers in regular contact. | Discuss requirements and feedback with users or classmates during reviews. |
| 5 | Support motivated team members and trust them to do the work. | Agree on responsibilities and let each teammate own a small task. |
| 6 | Communicate directly where possible. | Use a short conversation or call to resolve unclear requirements quickly. |
| 7 | Working software is the clearest sign of progress. | Track completed acceptance criteria and demonstrate the feature. |
| 8 | Work at a steady pace that can be maintained. | Select a realistic amount of work for each sprint and raise blockers early. |
| 9 | Keep improving technical quality and design. | Review code, run tests, and fix important defects before adding more features. |
| 10 | Keep the solution as simple as possible. | Build only the agreed student-project features and avoid unnecessary data collection. |
| 11 | Let the team organize how it completes the work. | Team members agree how to divide and review sprint tasks. |
| 12 | Regularly reflect and adjust how the team works. | Hold a retrospective and record one improvement for the next sprint. |
