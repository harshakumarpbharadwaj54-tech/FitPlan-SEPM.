**Date:** Enter the date you do the role-play

## Questions and notes

1. What fitness goals would you want FitPlan to help you organize?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “I want to build a regular fitness routine.”

2. Which days and how much time are usually available for activity?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “My available days change with my class schedule. I prefer shorter sessions.”

3. What makes generic workout plans difficult to follow?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “They may assume I have more time or experience than I do.”

4. What would you want to record about workouts?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “The activity, date, and how long I did it.”

5. What would you want to record about meals?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “A simple meal description and date.”

6. What information should a weekly plan consider?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “My goal, available days, preferences, and rest time.”

7. What should FitPlan do if it cannot safely validate a generated plan?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “It should not show it as accepted and should explain that it needs review.”

8. What information, if any, would you want to share with a trainer?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “Only the plan and logs I choose to share.”

9. What privacy or data controls would you expect?
   - Notes: Sample answer: “I should be able to control sharing and request deletion of my data.”

10. What would make FitPlan useful and easy to use?
    - Notes: Sample answer: “Clear screens, simple logging, and a plan that fits my schedule.”

## Summary

**Needs heard:** Sample only: flexible scheduling, simple logs, and control over sharing.

**Questions to clarify:** Sample only: which profile fields are actually needed?
